**Trying to find out whether electricity is off in your area, when it might return, or whom to call?** [PowerCutStatus.in](https://powercutstatus.in/) brings together locality-based outage checks, published utility shutdown information, complaint contacts, community observations, electricity guides and free household power calculators in one place.

> **Important:** PowerCutStatus.in is an independent electricity information service, **not** a government department, DISCOM, 24/7 control room or guaranteed live reading for every home. Coverage and source freshness vary by area and provider. A missing outage notice does **not** mean electricity is available. For a supply complaint, use the official utility serving your connection, often reachable through **1912**; save the complaint reference.

## What is PowerCutStatus.in?

PowerCutStatus.in helps people across India understand electricity interruptions and take the right next step. Rather than making users search many different electricity-board websites, it provides starting points for **city, neighbourhood, sector, feeder and six-digit PIN-code searches**; links to published **scheduled power shutdowns**; access to **provider information and complaint routes**; and practical guidance for **home electricity use, bills, backup, solar and wiring**.

The website aims to distinguish *official provider notices*, *community observations*, *planned maintenance schedules*, and *unknown or unverified status*. These are not interchangeable: a notice about a feeder does not prove that every home nearby is off, and a planned end time is not a guaranteed restoration time.

### Who can use it?

- **Residents and tenants** checking a neighbourhood outage and finding the right electricity company.
- **Families and remote workers** planning router, inverter or battery backup during recurring cuts.
- **Students and small businesses** estimating appliance electricity use, generator running costs or backup needs.
- **Homeowners and renters** learning how to read a bill, compare electricity schemes or prepare a service complaint.
- **Electricians and home renovators** using preliminary wiring, sanctioned-load and cable-size planning calculators (professional verification is still necessary).

## How to get the most out of the outage checker

1. **Search precisely.** On the [home page](https://powercutstatus.in/), enter your **city, area, sector or 6-digit PIN**. Select the matching locality; a city or PIN may cover multiple neighbourhoods or feeders.
2. **Match the correct area and utility.** Use the provider printed on your latest electricity bill; city boundaries and PIN codes do not always define a DISCOM service boundary.
3. **Read the evidence, not just the headline.** Look for affected locality, feeder, planned work dates, **source or observation time**, and whether the information is an official notice or a resident report.
4. **Understand unknown status.** If the site lacks fresh applicable evidence, electricity at your home **cannot be confirmed** from the site. Do not interpret a blank list as “power is on.”
5. **Treat restoration estimates cautiously.** A utility's planned shutdown end time may change. An estimate shown for another area may not apply to your connection.
6. **Check the official source when uncertain.** The relevant utility's own notice or customer service can be more suitable for an account-specific answer.
7. **Make an official complaint if needed.** Use the [contact directory](https://powercutstatus.in/electricity-complaint-numbers) and note the complaint number. A report or chat message on PowerCutStatus **does not register an official complaint**.
8. **Share local observations responsibly.** If reporting is available for your selected locality, use it accurately and without personal account details. Community reports are evidence to compare, not utility confirmation.

### What do the types of information mean?

| Information | Useful for | What it does **not** guarantee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Published planned shutdown | Checking a utility's proposed work window and named affected area | That supply is currently off, or returns at exactly the end time |
| Provider source check time | Knowing when the source was last accessed or updated | That your meter or home was checked then |
| Community observation | Seeing what someone in or near an area reported | Verified supply status for your exact connection |
| No matching notice / unknown | Understanding that evidence is insufficient | That there is no fault or power cut |
| Official complaint channel | Reporting a connection-specific issue to the provider | An instant repair or assured ETA |

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there a power cut in my area today?**  
Enter your city, locality or six-digit PIN at [PowerCutStatus.in](https://powercutstatus.in/), choose the exact area and examine the dated evidence. If nothing applies to your location, status remains uncertain; contact the utility on your bill for an account-specific check.

**When will electricity come back?**  
If an applicable official notice includes a planned end time or a utility provides an estimate, use it as a guide, **not a guarantee**. If there is no current applicable estimate, a reliable restoration time is unknown; contact the utility.

**Can I report a power cut without signing in?**  
Some locality pages support community observations. These do not register a supply complaint. Use the provider's official channel for faults affecting your connection.

**How do I find my electricity complaint or lineman number?**  
Open the [complaint-number directory](https://powercutstatus.in/electricity-complaint-numbers), select your state and area, and confirm your actual supplier. Records may contain office lines or complaint centres rather than individual lineman numbers; staff rosters can become outdated.

**How do I estimate my monthly electricity units?**  
Use the [consumption calculator](https://powercutstatus.in/tools/power-consumption-calculator) with each appliance's approximate operating watts and daily usage hours. One unit equals one kilowatt-hour (kWh). Replace sample data with your actual equipment values for a better estimate.

**Can I pay my bill on PowerCutStatus?**  
The [bill checker](https://powercutstatus.in/electricity-bill-check) helps you find the relevant official electricity-provider service. Confirm its identity before entering consumer information or paying.

**Does PowerCutStatus guarantee live status in every area?**  
No. Official data access, published notices, community coverage and locality mappings are incomplete in some places. An unknown state is more informative than an unsupported “power available” claim.

**What should I do about a fallen wire, sparks or damaged electrical equipment?**  
Keep away, warn others from a safe distance and contact the electricity provider's official emergency channel. Do not touch wires, poles or damaged equipment. A website or community chat cannot handle an emergency.
